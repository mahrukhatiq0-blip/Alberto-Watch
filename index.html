About Alberto

Craft That Outlasts Time

Alberto began with one watchmaker and one belief: a watch is a legacy. Every dial is finished in deep blue, black and silver, inspired by the great classics of Swiss watchmaking: divers, chronographs, dual-time and date models. From a child’s first watch to a gentleman’s heirloom, each piece is built to be worn, loved and passed on.
